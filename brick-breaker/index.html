ANON BRICK BREAKER

Drag (or use ← →) to move the golden paddle. Tap or press Space to launch. Every brick is a piece of ANON NFT art: break them all to reveal the full artwork and move to the next level. 100 levels, and every one gets harder.
You start with 3 lives and keep them from level to level. Every 10th level gives a bonus life (max 5).
Gold power-ups: W wider paddle · M multi-ball · S slow ball · ♥ extra life.
Dodge the red drops: − shrink paddle · ⚡ fast ball · ⇄ reversed controls · ☠ lose a life.

Continue is practice: score starts at 0 and is not posted. Leaderboard runs start at level 1.
