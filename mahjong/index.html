Match pairs of identical tiles to clear the board. A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and its left or right side is open (free tiles are brighter). Tap two matching free tiles to remove them. Stuck? Use 🔀 Shuffle (Easy 5, Medium 2, Hard 1) or 💡 Hint (Easy unlimited, Medium 3, Hard none). Every deal can be cleared.
