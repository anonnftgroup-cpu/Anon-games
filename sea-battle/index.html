Choose your ANON
Difficulty

Both fleets hide 5 ships (Shogun 5, Ronin 4, Ninja 3, Shadow 3, Kunai 2). Tap the enemy grid to fire: ✕ is a hit, a dot is a miss. You take turns with the CPU. Sink all 5 enemy ships first to win. Fewer shots score more. Don't like your fleet? Tap 🔀 New fleet before your first shot.
