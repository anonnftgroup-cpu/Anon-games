PYRAMID HOP

Choose your ANON
Difficulty

Tap the diagonal buttons, swipe diagonally, or use the arrow keys (↑ up-right, ← up-left, ↓ down-left, → down-right). Every hop changes the cube top. Turn the whole pyramid to the target colour to clear the level. Later levels need two hops per cube, then flip back if you hop again! Dodge the skull orbs and the ghost chaser, and don't hop off the edge.
