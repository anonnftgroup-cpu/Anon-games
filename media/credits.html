Credits & rights

ANON Cinema & Radio is a free, curated hub from ANON NFTs. We only list material that is public domain in the UK, Creative Commons licensed for commercial use, or embedded from the rights holder's own official channel. Every item is re-checked on a schedule. If you believe an item is listed in error, contact us via anonnfts.com and we will remove it promptly.

Catalogue last updated 2026-10-19. Ratings come from Wikidata (review score, P444) where one exists. Age ratings come from the BBFC where a classification matches the exact film or series; otherwise the item is shown as "Not rated" with our own content note.

ANON Cinema: films

Every film below is public domain in the UK. Under UK law a film's copyright lasts until 70 years after the death of the last of its principal director, screenplay and dialogue authors, and the composer of specially written music. For every title, all of these people died before 1956. We only use archive.org copies with no added soundtrack, so there are no modern score rights. The files stream from the Internet Archive's player.
