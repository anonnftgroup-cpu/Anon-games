GALAXY SHOOTER

Choose your ANON
Difficulty

Your ANON ship fires by itself. Slide your finger anywhere on the screen (or use ◀ ▶ / arrow keys) to move. Shadow drones fly in formation and swoop down at you: divers score double, crowned drones take 2 hits. Catch pink gems for a double shot. Clear a wave for a bonus. Easy has 4 lives, Medium and Hard 3.
