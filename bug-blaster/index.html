BUG BLASTER

Choose your ANON
Difficulty

Drag anywhere on the screen (or use the arrows / pad) to move your ANON around the gold zone at the bottom. Your blaster fires on its own. Every segment you hit turns into a stone and splits the chain. Heads are worth 100, body segments 10, and the jumping grumpy spider 300 to 900 (more when it's close). Clear the chain to start the next, faster wave. You have 3 lives.
