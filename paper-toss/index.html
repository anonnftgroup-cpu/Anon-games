PAPER TOSS

Choose your ANON

Swipe up (flick) from anywhere to throw the crumpled sketch: a longer swipe throws further, and swiping to the side aims left or right. Watch the green wind arrow: it pushes the paper sideways, so aim against it. Every throw you bin moves you up a level, and the bin gets further away and the wind gets stronger. Streaks earn bonus points. 3 misses and you're out. Keys: ◀ ▶ aim, ▲ ▼ power, Space throws. The doodles are made-up joke sketches, not real projects.
