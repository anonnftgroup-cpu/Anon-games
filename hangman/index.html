HANGMAN

Choose your ANON
Difficulty

Guess the word one letter at a time (tap the keys or type). Every right letter reveals more of the hidden ANON NFT. Every wrong letter blows out a lantern. Solve the word to move on to the next one. The run ends when all the lanterns are out. Easy: 8 lanterns, shorter words. Medium: 6. Hard: 5 lanterns, long words, triple points.
