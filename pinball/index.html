ANON GALAXY PINBALL

Table: ANON Galaxy
  • Flippers: tap the left or right half of the table.
  • Launch: hold the right side (or swipe down), then let go. Longer hold = stronger shot.
  • Missions: collect pink gems, hit the crown targets, spell A-N-O-N and escape the shadow forest to rise from Shadow Recruit to ANON King.
  • Every third mission starts Galaxy Multiball. Loop the forest during multiball for a jackpot.
  • Nudge to save a ball, but nudge too often and you'll TILT.
Keyboard: Z / M or ← / → flippers · Space to launch · N nudge · P pause
