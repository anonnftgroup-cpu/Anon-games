ANON BLOCK DROP

ANON art blocks fall into the 9-wide vault. Fill a whole row to clear it. Drag left/right to move, tap to turn, swipe down to drop. Keys: ← → move, ↑ or X turn, ↓ soft drop, Space drop.
Rows: 1 = 100, 2 = 300, 3 = 500, 4 = 800 × level. Every 10 rows the level goes up and blocks fall faster.
