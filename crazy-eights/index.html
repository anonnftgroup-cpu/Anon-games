ANON CRAZY 8S

Choose your ANON
Difficulty

Be first to empty your hand. Play a card that matches the top card's suit or number (raised cards are playable). 8s are wild: play one any time and pick the new suit. Can't play? Tap the deck to draw one card. Win to score: you get points for every card left in your rivals' hands (8 = 50, J/Q/K = 10).
