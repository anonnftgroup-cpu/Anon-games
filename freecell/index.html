ANON FREECELL

Choose your ANON
Difficulty

Move every card to the 4 foundations (A to K by suit). Tap a card to pick it up, then tap where it goes (green outline = allowed). Tap a picked card again to auto-move it. Columns build down in alternating colours. Free cells hold one card each. Easy: 4 cells, unlimited undo. Medium: 4 cells, 5 undos. Hard: 3 cells, no undo.
