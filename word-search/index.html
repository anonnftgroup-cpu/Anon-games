ANON WORD SEARCH

Choose your ANON
Difficulty

Drag your finger across the letters in a straight line to circle a word from the list. Easy: 8×8, words go across or down, 3 minutes. Medium: 10×10, adds diagonals, 4 minutes. Hard: 12×12, words can run backwards in any direction, 5 minutes. Every word scores, and finding them all adds a bonus for the time left.
